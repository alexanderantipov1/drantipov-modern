What Are All-on-4® Dental Implants?

All-on-4® is a full mouth dental implant solution designed for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth or whose remaining teeth can no longer be saved.

Instead of replacing every tooth with an individual implant, four strategically positioned implants are placed into the jawbone to support an entire fixed arch of replacement teeth.

Depending on your anatomy and bone quality, some patients may receive additional implants, such as All-on-5® or All-on-6®, to provide even greater support and long-term stability.

The goal remains the same:

Restore a complete smile with permanent, non-removable teeth.

Is It Really Possible to Get Teeth in One Day?

One of the biggest misconceptions about dental implants is that patients must wait months before receiving teeth.

While healing is still required, many qualified patients can receive a fixed temporary bridge on the very same day their implants are placed.

This treatment is commonly known as:

Teeth in a Day
Same-Day Dental Implants
Immediate Teeth
Immediate Loading

Instead of leaving surgery with removable dentures, patients leave with a functional, natural-looking smile attached directly to their implants.

Who Is a Candidate for Same-Day Dental Implants?

Not every patient qualifies for immediate loading.

Successful treatment depends on several important factors, including:

Bone density
Bone volume
Overall oral health
Implant stability
Medical history
Bite forces

Careful patient selection is one of the most important steps in achieving predictable long-term success.

During your consultation, advanced 3D CT imaging allows the surgical team to determine whether same-day teeth are an appropriate option.

Understanding Immediate Extraction, Immediate Placement, and Immediate Loading

These three terms are often confused, but each describes a different stage of treatment.

Immediate Extraction

If damaged or failing teeth cannot be saved, they are removed during the same appointment as implant surgery.

Immediate Implant Placement

Immediately after the teeth are removed, dental implants are placed into the jawbone whenever conditions allow.

This reduces the number of surgical procedures and preserves existing bone whenever possible.

Immediate Loading

Once the implants achieve sufficient stability, a fixed temporary bridge is attached to the implants on the very same day.

Patients leave with teeth instead of waiting months wearing removable dentures.

More Than Replacing Teeth—Restoring Your Entire Smile

One of the greatest advantages of full arch dental implants is that treatment restores much more than missing teeth.

Patients who have worn dentures for years often experience:

Bone loss
Collapsed facial appearance
Loss of lip support
Reduced facial height
Wrinkles around the mouth
Difficulty chewing
Reduced confidence

As teeth are lost, the upper and lower jaws gradually move closer together.

This loss of vertical dimension can create the sunken facial appearance commonly associated with long-term denture wear.

A properly designed full arch restoration rebuilds the missing support that has been lost over time.

By restoring the proper bite height, many patients notice a fuller facial profile, improved lip support, and a more youthful appearance.

What Is a Hybrid Bridge?

Many patients hear the term "hybrid bridge" without understanding what it means.

A hybrid bridge is a fixed full arch restoration that combines multiple materials into one strong prosthesis.

Typically, it consists of:

A titanium framework for strength
Custom-designed replacement teeth
Gum-colored materials that replace missing tissue

Unlike traditional dentures, the bridge remains securely attached to the implants.

Only your dental team removes it during maintenance appointments.

The Benefits of Fixed Dental Implants

Compared to removable dentures, implant-supported teeth offer significant advantages.

Non-Removable Teeth

Your new teeth stay securely in place.

There is no slipping, shifting, adhesives, or removing them at night.

Better Chewing Function

Because implants are anchored into the jawbone, patients can enjoy a much stronger bite and eat a wider variety of foods comfortably.

Bone Preservation

When implants stimulate the jawbone during chewing, they help reduce the bone loss that naturally occurs after tooth loss.

This is one of the biggest long-term advantages over traditional dentures.

Improved Facial Appearance

Restoring lost bone and facial support creates a healthier, fuller smile while reducing the collapsed appearance caused by missing teeth.

Increased Confidence

Perhaps the greatest benefit is psychological.

Patients smile more, speak with confidence, enjoy social situations, and no longer worry about loose dentures or missing teeth.

What Happens During the Procedure?

Every patient receives a customized treatment plan, but the general process includes:

Comprehensive consultation and CT scan
Digital treatment planning
Removal of failing teeth (if necessary)
Placement of four or more dental implants
Attachment of a fixed temporary bridge
Healing period while implants integrate with the jawbone
Delivery of the final custom restoration

For many patients, the transformation begins the very same day.

Are All-on-4® Dental Implants Permanent?

Dental implants themselves are designed to become a permanent part of the jawbone.

The temporary bridge worn immediately after surgery is later replaced with a stronger, long-term final restoration after healing is complete.

With proper home care, routine maintenance, and regular professional cleanings, dental implants can provide decades of reliable function.
