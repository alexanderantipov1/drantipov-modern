Here are the full specs for the review banner (the slim navy "740+ patients" band that sits right under the hero). You can rebuild it anywhere with these details.

What it is
A dark, rounded bar with three things on the left (a stack of review-platform circles, a big rating number, five gold stars, and a small caption) and a "TRUSTED ON" badge group on the right. On the live site it sits inside a full-width navy strip below the hero, and the caption is a button that opens a slide-out panel of reviews.

Layout structure
Outer row: left group and right group, spaced apart (justify-between), wraps on small screens.
Left group:
Overlapping circle stack (Google, Facebook, Yelp), each circle slightly overlapping the next (negative spacing, about -8px).
Rating number, then five stars, on one line.
Caption line below it ("Average rating from 740+ patients").
Right group: a vertical divider, the label "TRUSTED ON", then three small mono icons. This group is hidden on mobile, shown from medium screens up.
Colors
Banner background (standalone card version): dark navy #0a1929 at 80% opacity with a subtle backdrop blur.
Under-hero strip background: solid #0a1929.
Border: white at 15% opacity (rgba(255,255,255,0.15)).
Stars: fill and stroke #F5A623 (gold). The slider/pill version uses #F59E0B.
Rating number text: white.
Caption text: white at 65% opacity; the highlighted "740+ patients" part is solid white, underlined.
"TRUSTED ON" label: white at 45% opacity.
Right-side mono icons: white at 55% opacity.
Platform circles: Google circle is white, Facebook circle #1877F2, Yelp circle #FF1A1A with a white bold "Y". Each circle has a 2px ring in the banner background color so they read as separate.
Sizing and spacing
Card padding: about 12px to 14px vertical, 16px to 20px horizontal. Rounded corners about 16px (rounded-2xl).
Under-hero strip: about 20px vertical padding, inside your normal page container.
Platform circles: 32px on mobile, 36px from small screens up.
Rating number: about 18px to 20px, bold.
Stars: about 14px to 16px.
Caption: about 12px to 14px.
"TRUSTED ON" label: 12px, semibold, wide letter spacing (about 0.18em).
Content and data
Default rating: 4.7 for the standalone banner; the under-hero version passes 4.9.
Default caption: "Average rating from 740+ patients."
Star count: always 5 filled stars (it is a visual trust mark, not a half-star meter).
Platforms shown: Google, Facebook, Yelp.
Props (so it stays reusable)
rating (string), default "4.7".
caption (text or element), default the "740+ patients" line.
trustedLabel (string), default "TRUSTED ON".
className for layout overrides.
Behavior
The caption can be a plain line or a button. On the live site it is a button that opens a left-side slide-out sheet listing reviews (name, platform icon, time, star row, review text) with a "Load More" button that reveals 4 more at a time.
Accessibility: the star row has an aria-label like "4.9 out of 5 stars"; icons are marked aria-hidden.
Responsive: the whole bar wraps on small screens, and the right-side "TRUSTED ON" group is hidden below the medium breakpoint to keep it clean.
