## What Do Full Mouth Dental Implants in Elk Grove Replace?

**Full-mouth dental implant treatment restores an entire upper arch, lower arch, or both with an implant-supported replacement, based on a patient’s needs.** The phrase describes the scope of treatment, not one universal procedure. A plan may involve a fixed full-arch restoration or an implant-supported denture. In other situations, individual implants may be planned for separate tooth sites.

In each approach, implants are placed in the jaw to support replacement teeth. A [dental implant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_implant) is a fixture that can integrate with the surrounding bone, providing support for a prosthesis. Treatment is intended to address missing or failing teeth and help restore everyday function and confidence. The design and expected results depend on each person’s clinical situation.

### What is the difference between full-arch implants and replacing every tooth individually?

A full-arch restoration replaces a row of teeth with one prosthesis supported by dental implants. Instead of placing an implant at every tooth position, the treatment uses implants to anchor the replacement arch. Individual implants, by contrast, replace teeth at separate sites, with each replacement planned for its own location. The appropriate approach depends on examination findings, the condition of the teeth and supporting tissues, and the patient’s goals.
