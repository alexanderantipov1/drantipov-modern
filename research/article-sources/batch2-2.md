### How do full-arch implants and implant-supported dentures differ?

A fixed full-arch restoration is secured to dental implants and is designed to remain in place during daily use. An implant-supported denture, including a snap-in option, attaches to implants for support but can be removed by the patient for cleaning. Both approaches replace teeth, but they differ in removability and how the restoration is cared for each day.

Neither option is right for everyone. Your goals for stability and daily care matter, as do the clinical findings. When reviewing an estimate, look for the restoration type and how it fits into the overall plan. A fixed restoration and a removable implant-supported denture aren’t interchangeable, even when both replace a full arch.

They shouldn’t be assumed in advance: the recommendation depends on examination findings and diagnostic imaging. Materials and the design of the final restoration also influence the plan.
