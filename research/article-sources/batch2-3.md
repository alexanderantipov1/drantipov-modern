## How Implant-Supported Teeth Can Replace Traditional Dentures

Implant-supported treatment changes how replacement teeth are held in place. Rather than resting only on the gums, a restoration connects to implant posts placed in the jaw. ### How do implant-supported dentures work?

Implants can help retain a removable denture, such as a snap-in denture. The denture attaches to the implants for added support during wear, but you still remove it for cleaning. That differs from a conventional denture, which rests on the gums without implant retention. Added support doesn’t guarantee a particular level of comfort or fit, which varies by individual.

The number and placement of implants are determined as part of an individual treatment plan. An assessment considers your oral health and the restoration being planned, rather than assuming the same arrangement works for everyone.

### How do fixed full-arch teeth differ?

A fixed full-arch prosthesis attaches to implants and isn’t removed by the patient as part of a daily cleaning routine. It’s cared for in place, with professional follow-up and home care guided by the dental team. All-on-4 is one full-arch treatment approach, not a universal solution or an automatic fit for every patient. A clinician needs to assess whether it aligns with your needs.

**In short: removable implant-supported teeth come out for cleaning; fixed implant-supported teeth stay in place and are cared for without patient removal.** Both use implants for support, but they involve different routines and treatment considerations.
