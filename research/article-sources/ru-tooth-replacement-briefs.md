## Editorial treatment

Preserved the clinical distinctions in the briefs: bridges do not automatically require root canal treatment; implants do not prevent all bone changes; tooth movement is possible rather than inevitable; wisdom teeth, planned orthodontic space closure, and selected shortened arches may not need replacement; delayed implantation can remain possible; immediate placement is not mandatory; ridge preservation does not stop remodeling completely.
