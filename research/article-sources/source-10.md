## A practical step-by-step plan for changing implant providers

A structured transition can make changing providers feel more manageable. Move in stages, and don’t assume that records, scheduling, or a previous treatment plan will transfer automatically.

1. **Identify your goals.** Write down why you’re considering a change, such as wanting clearer communication, easier travel, or another opinion. Note the questions you still need answered.
2. **Request available records.** Ask your current office about its release process and whether it can send records and imaging directly to the new surgeon. Keep track of what you receive and what’s still missing.
3. **Book an evaluation.** When you contact the new office, explain what treatment you’ve already had, what remains planned, and whether you have upcoming appointments. Ask whether records are needed before the visit or if the team can help identify what’s missing.
4. **Compare recommendations.** Review the new surgeon’s findings, proposed options, and any differences from the earlier plan. Ask for clarification before deciding.
5. **Confirm next steps.** Make sure you understand who coordinates follow-up, what needs to happen next, and how to contact the team with questions.

Ask about timing and coordination rather than assuming the change will be immediate. The transition may involve record review, an evaluation, or additional assessment before the surgeon can recommend a plan. Keep a written log of questions, records received, and decisions after each conversation. It can help you stay organized and spot anything you need to clarify.
