## What to Ask at a Consultation Before Choosing an Implant-and-Denture Dentist

A consultation should leave you with a clearer picture of your options, not pressure to choose before you’re ready. If you’re comparing a dentist that does implants and dentures near me, bring questions about the diagnosis, care team, follow-up, and full cost. A provider should explain alternatives in language you understand and show how the recommendation reflects your health, goals, and priorities.

1. **What findings inform your recommendation?** Ask what the evaluation shows and how it affects your options. If you’re considering implants, dentures, or both, ask which alternatives are reasonable to discuss and why.
2. **How does the plan reflect my priorities?** Explain what matters most to you, such as a removable or fixed option, care routines, or treatment timing. Ask how those preferences shape the recommendation and what trade-offs to consider.
3. **Who handles each stage of care?** Clarify who evaluates and plans treatment, who places implants, and who designs or fits the prosthesis. If more than one clinician is involved, ask how they coordinate and whom to contact with questions.
4. **What appointments and follow-up should I expect?** Ask the provider to outline the steps for your proposed plan, including follow-up visits and how concerns or complications would be addressed. The schedule should be specific to your treatment, not a general promise.
5. **Can I review a written, itemized treatment plan?** Request written fees and ask what each amount includes, such as the implant or prosthesis, related procedures, and follow-up care. Clarify which services could involve additional fees and ask the practice to explain any unfamiliar terms.
6. **How might insurance or financing apply?** Ask the practice which benefits it can help you review, then confirm coverage, exclusions, and limits with your insurer. If you’re considering financing, ask whether it’s available and request the current terms in writing. Don’t assume an estimate or coverage applies until it’s confirmed.
