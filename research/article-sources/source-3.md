### The Biological Advantage: Preventing Jawbone Resorption

Your jawbone needs the constant stimulation of tooth roots to remain dense and strong. When teeth are missing, the bone begins to dissolve because it no longer has a functional purpose. Bone resorption is the physiological process where the body breaks down and absorbs bone tissue in the jaw, leading to a sunken facial appearance and a weakened bite. Dental implants are the only solution that stops this process by mimicking the natural root. By preserving this bone, you maintain your natural facial structure and avoid the collapsed look that often accompanies long-term denture use.

### Systemic Health and Nutrition: More Than Just a Smile

Good health starts with the ability to eat a varied, nutritious diet. Many seniors with missing teeth or loose dentures subconsciously shift toward soft, processed foods that are easy to chew but low in vital nutrients. This change can lead to chronic digestive issues and unintended weight loss. Implants restore your full chewing power, allowing you to enjoy proteins and fibrous vegetables again. Research continues to highlight the link between oral health and systemic conditions. Maintaining a healthy, stable mouth helps reduce the inflammatory load on your body, supporting heart health and cognitive function as you age.
