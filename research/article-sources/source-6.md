### The Anatomy of a Premium Dental Implant

A premium dental implant consists of three distinct components designed to work in harmony with your body. The first is the post, a small screw made of titanium or zirconia that is placed into the jawbone. Next is the abutment, which serves as a connector between the post and the final restoration. Finally, the custom-made crown or bridge provides the visible, functional tooth. The success of these parts depends on the [biological process of osseointegration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_implant), which is the vital foundation where your natural bone fuses directly to the implant surface. Choosing between titanium and zirconia often depends on your specific aesthetic goals and any metal sensitivities you might have.

### Single and Multi-Site Implants

If you only have one or two gaps in your smile, a single tooth implant is the gold standard for preservation. Traditional bridges require grinding down healthy neighboring teeth to act as anchors, which can compromise their long-term health. An implant stands alone, acting as an independent root. When you lose a tooth, the surrounding bone begins to resorb because it no longer receives stimulation from chewing. This process can lead to a "sunken" facial appearance over time. Single implants halt this recession, maintaining your natural facial structure and youthful contours. Each crown is custom-fabricated to match the shade and translucency of your existing teeth.
