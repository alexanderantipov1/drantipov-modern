## Fixed full-arch implants or implant-supported dentures: how do costs compare?

These options differ in how the teeth are used, cleaned, and described in a treatment plan. Fixed full-arch restorations stay in place during daily use, while implant-supported dentures, including snap-in dentures, are designed to be removed for cleaning. Estimates may cover different prostheses and treatment stages, so compare the proposed care, not just the total. For a patient-friendly overview of implant treatment, see [Cleveland Clinic's Guide to Dental Implants](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/10903-dental-implants).

| Comparison point | Fixed full-arch restoration | Removable implant-supported denture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Removability | Designed to remain in place during daily use. | Designed to be removed by the patient for cleaning. |
| Maintenance | Ask how to clean around the restoration and what professional maintenance is recommended. | Ask how to clean the denture and attachments, and what ongoing care is recommended. |
| Daily use | Worn in place. Ask how the proposed design fits your needs and routine. | Placed and removed by the patient. Discuss whether this approach suits your preferences. |
| Estimate inclusions | Confirm the planned prosthesis, temporary teeth, final restoration, and follow-up provisions. | Confirm the denture, attachment components, any temporary teeth, and follow-up provisions. |

The main practical difference is whether you remove the prosthesis yourself.
