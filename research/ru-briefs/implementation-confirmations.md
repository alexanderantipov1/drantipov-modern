# Seven Russian service pages: outstanding practice confirmations

The seven additive routes are implemented with educational Russian copy, not statements that a particular procedure or product is currently offered. Existing parent routes remain unchanged. The copy incorporates the clinical corrections in `complete-review.md`; implementation is not clinician approval or verification of a provider's qualifications.

## Claims not independently confirmed and therefore not published as practice facts

- Current California licence, applicable scope/permissions, current ABOMS status, injectable-specific training, and identity of the actual treating practitioner. An existing biography is an evidence-inventory entry, not independent verification.
- The basis for “25+ years” of experience and whether that experience concerns surgery, implant treatment or aesthetic injections.
- Neodent Elite Practitioner status, year, issuing evidence and exact meaning. No award, manufacturer recognition or superiority claim was added.
- Current availability of each specific implant configuration or pterygoid technique, the operating/orthodontic-prosthetic team, procedure locations, availability of general anaesthesia or IV sedation, and which stages actually take place in the same office.
- Current availability of aesthetic injections, whether the toxin is BOTOX Cosmetic/onabotulinumtoxinA or another product, which zones are offered, and the exact lip-filler SKU. Product instructions are discussed as educational evidence, not evidence of the practice's stock or services.
- Routine use of surgical guides, clinician-approved stage timing, local scheduling, and the construction details of particular bridges.
- Actual prices, financing eligibility, the contents of any free consultation/CT offer, and restrictions. Booking language is neutral; no free offer, financing promise or invented price was added.
- Practice-specific outcome data, unsupported success percentages and implant-lifespan claims. No “95%+”, “97%+” or 25-year guarantee is published as a treatment outcome. Pterygoid reviews are linked with their methodological limitations rather than used as a clinic metric.
- Approval of attributed doctor quotations. Quotes were omitted, not anonymised or fabricated.
- Case identity, implant counts, product/technique, before/after dates, image rights and patient consent. No procedure-specific gallery or testimonial was invented from a general gallery, office photo or unrelated case.
- Emergency preparedness, out-of-hours contact arrangements and availability of a particular hyaluronidase product. No claim of readiness or guaranteed reversal was made; patient-facing emergency advice is provided independently.

## Existing-site evidence inventory (not verification)

The review identifies `SurgeonAuthority.tsx`, the team page, `About.tsx` / `RuAbout.tsx`, `courses.ts`, the existing EN/RU service constants and consultation forms as places where related statements already occur. They establish only the existence of earlier marketing or educational statements. They do not authenticate qualifications, medical outcomes, current service/product availability or an approved commercial offer.

## Required practice review before treating copy as medically approved

1. Have the responsible clinician review Russian anatomical terminology, selection criteria, MRONJ/medication language, procedural alternatives, risks and emergency instructions.
2. Verify each actual service, provider, product, current instruction, age restriction and permitted use. Adjust educational text only from documented facts; do not infer service availability from a past case.
3. Confirm clinical and prosthetic logistics, realistic scheduling, written estimates and follow-up arrangements. Explain the fallback if same-day temporary loading is not safe.
4. For injections, confirm informed-consent materials, escalation arrangements and staff training. A hyaluronidase supply does not guarantee reversal of vascular injury or restoration of vision.
5. Supply approved, procedure-specific cases and rights before adding optional galleries, results or quotations.

No application workflow was started or restarted as part of this implementation. Runtime and visual verification belong to the owning agent.
