Frequently asked questions — Loomis patients

How far is Dr. Antipov's office from Loomis?+

Our Roseville office is approximately 7 miles from Loomis — about 12 min by car. I-80 West to Douglas Boulevard East (about 12 minutes).

How much do dental implants cost for Loomis patients?+

A single dental implant (implant, abutment, and crown) typically runs $4,000–$6,000. Full-arch restoration such as All-on-4 generally ranges from $25,000–$35,000 per arch. Your exact cost depends on bone volume, the number of implants, and whether grafting is needed — we provide a clear written estimate at your free dental implant consultation, and offer financing through CareCredit and Cherry.

Do you accept dental insurance for patients from Loomis?+

We work with most major PPO dental insurance plans. For surgical procedures (jaw surgery, sleep apnea MMA, facial trauma), much of the treatment is covered under medical insurance — we'll verify both before your consultation.

Are emergency oral surgery appointments available for Loomis patients?+

Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency on-call service for urgent oral and maxillofacial surgery needs. Call (916) 783-2110 — patients from the entire Sacramento Metro area are welcome for emergencies.

What makes Dr. Antipov different from other oral surgeons near Loomis?+

Dr. Antipov is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (ABOMS), with 25+ years of experience and hospital privileges at Sutter Roseville, Sutter General, Mercy General, and Mercy San Juan. He has personally performed every surgery since opening his Roseville practice in 2008.

Can I get a free dental implant consultation if I live in Loomis?+

Yes — a complimentary dental implant consultation with 3D CT scan is available to new patients considering implants, including those traveling from Loomis. The scan takes about 10 minutes and provides a complete view of your jaw, sinuses, and bone density for accurate treatment planning. Restrictions apply.
